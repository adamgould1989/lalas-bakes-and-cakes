Party Experiences

LaLa's Party Kitchen & LaLa's Social Kitchen offer beautifully hosted dessert decorating experiences for children, teens and adults.

From colourful children's celebrations to stylish grown-up socials, our experiences are designed to bring people together through creativity, sweet treats and stress-free hosting.

Every event is fully hosted and thoughtfully styled, with all ingredients, equipment and setup included — allowing guests to relax, get creative and enjoy the experience.

Whether you're planning a child's birthday party, a girls' night, baby shower, hen party or special celebration, we create memorable experiences that feel personal, relaxed and completely hassle-free.

Based around fun, creativity and beautifully presented desserts, our parties are suitable for all experience levels and designed to leave every guest with something delicious to take home.
